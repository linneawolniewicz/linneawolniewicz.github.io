I conducted this work as my Masters project in 2024, presented it as a scheduled talk at the SPAICE Conference 2024, a poster at the 37th NeurIPS Machine Learning for the Physical Sciences Workshop, and have now submitted a new version of the work to the Journal on Geophysical Research Machine Learning and Computation.

In this work, I train a surrogate neural likelihood model to accelerate Hamiltonian Monte Carlo sampling from the posterior over parameters describing the transport of galactic cosmic rays through our solar system.
