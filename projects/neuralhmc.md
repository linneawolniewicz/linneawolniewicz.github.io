I conducted this work as part of my master's project in 2024, presented it as a talk at the SPAICE Conference in 2024, and presented it as a poster at the 37th NeurIPS Machine Learning for the Physical Sciences Workshop in 2023. The updated paper was published in the Journal of Geophysical Research: Machine Learning and Computation on September 12, 2026.

In this work, I train a surrogate neural likelihood model to accelerate Hamiltonian Monte Carlo sampling from the posterior over parameters describing the transport of galactic cosmic rays through our solar system.
